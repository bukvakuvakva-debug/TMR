SUBJECT PROFILE

WHO IS TMR?

PROFILE_IMAGE_MISSING

The Mexican Runner is the online alias of Piotr Delgado Kusielczuk, a speedrunner known for his dedication to classic video games.

Specializing in retro gaming, particularly Nintendo Entertainment System titles, TMR became widely recognized for taking on incredibly difficult games and completing massive gaming challenges.

His passion for gaming, persistence and connection with the speedrunning community helped turn a personal challenge into an unforgettable achievement.

His best-known project, NESMania, involved completing every officially licensed NES game released in the NTSC and PAL libraries.

// THE BEGINNING OF A HUGE CHALLENGE

What began as an ambitious idea eventually became one of the most remarkable achievements in retro gaming history.

TMR started his NESMania journey in 2014. The objective was simple to describe but incredibly difficult to accomplish: beat every officially licensed game in the NES NTSC and PAL libraries.

On February 26, 2017, he completed the challenge, becoming the first person to finish all 714 games in the project.

The achievement earned him recognition from Guinness World Records.
